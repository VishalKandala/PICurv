- Examples: every shipped case and the master template write their initial condition as `properties.initial_conditions.source`. `mode: generated` and its built-ins remain accepted. The wall-seeded channel and duct cases are a mean source plus the generator's fluctuations, so their bulk velocity carries the midpoint rule's error (2e-5 to 1.25e-3 on the shipped grids) instead of being normalized to exactly 1; `initial_flux` cases latch that value (7e-5 and 2e-4). `bent_channel` projects its initial field.
- Initial conditions (experimental): a generator may stand alone as `source: {generator, params}`; its velocity matches `mode: generated` bit for bit. `project` now defaults to true for every `sum`, as documented, including one with no generator.
- Fixed: a `source` initial condition printed `Unknown Initial Condition` in the startup banner. A generator inside a `source` was not planned for `picurv precompute`, and its relative `config_file` and `script` were not resolved.
