- Inlets (experimental): `prescribed_velocity` is the one inlet handler. Its `source` may be data as well as expressions: `type: file`, `generated` or `field_slice`, the profiles `prescribed_flow` took under `params.source`, now on the entry itself, with `scale` applied at staging and `bulk_velocity` rescaling them each step. `constant_velocity`, `parabolic` and `prescribed_flow` are removed; a case naming one is refused with the equivalent source (`{components: normal, expression: <speed>}` for a uniform inlet, `"16*<v_max>*xi*(1-xi)*eta*(1-eta)"` for the parabola). Newton-Krylov momentum accepts the inlet. The master template's `-Eta` inlet, which named `vx` on a face that read `vy`, now delivers its 1.5 m/s.
