- The IEM mixing target is now bounded and conservative (EXPERIMENTAL): each cell's linear reconstruction m + ζ g·(x − x_c) uses a mean and centroid weighted by each particle's relaxation weight 1 − exp(−ω dt), which keeps every cell's Psi total for any ζ, and a per-cell slope factor ζ in [0, 1], the largest that keeps every target within the extrema of all particles' Psi at the start of the step (Jenny et al. 2001; Viswanathan, Wang & Pope 2011). Psi no longer leaves [0, 1] near sharp fronts; the limiter adds about 0.2% effective diffusivity at C 2-4 against the unlimited target and about 7% step time on a coupled case.
