- Initial conditions: `properties.initial_conditions` takes `source` and `constraints` alone. `mode`, `generator`, `params`, `field`, `source_file` and the built-in `zero`, `constant`, `streamwise_constant` and `poiseuille` are refused, each naming its replacement: an expression source, `source: {generator, params}`, or `source: {type: file, field, path}`. Existing cases must be edited. The solver's `-finit`, `-ucont_*`, `-flow_direction` and `-ic_velocity_physical` options are gone.
