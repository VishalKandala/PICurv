- `examples/periodic_test/driven_duct/tools/cross_section_profile.py` reduces the
  duct's statistics window to the comparisons its acceptance criteria name: the
  wall- and corner-bisector profiles in wall units, the cross-section's mean velocity
  and Reynolds stresses for the secondary flow, and the wall-shear distribution. By
  default it folds the cross-section over its eight symmetry images. Both shipped
  reducers now also work from the copies `picurv init` places in a case.
