- Expressions: a series `sum(k, lo, hi, body)` (integer bounds, up to 1000000 terms, nested up to four deep), Bessel functions of integer order `besselj(n, x)` and `bessely(n, x)`, and the Kelvin functions `ber(x)` and `bei(x)`, in velocity sources and particle values. `picurv` validates the special functions but leaves their evaluation to the solver, so `ic_gen` expressions cannot use them. The initial-conditions page gives exact rectangular-duct Poiseuille and Womersley pipe-flow sources written with them.
