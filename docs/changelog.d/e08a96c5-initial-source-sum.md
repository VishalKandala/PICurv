- Initial conditions (experimental): `properties.initial_conditions.source` may be a `sum` of velocity sources and at most one generator that writes a `Ucat`, such as a mean profile plus the fluctuations of `channel_spectral_velocity`. A `project` constraint, on by default for a sum, makes the initial field divergence-free before the first step with the time step's own pressure projection. `channel_spectral_velocity` and `duct_spectral_velocity` accept a streamwise axis running from an inlet to an outlet, and `bulk_velocity: 0` for the fluctuations alone.
