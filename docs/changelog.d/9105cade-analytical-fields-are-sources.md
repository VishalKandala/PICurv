
- Analytical fields: with `eulerian_field_source: analytical`, the field is the case's `properties.initial_conditions.source`, re-evaluated each step it depends on `t`; an optional `pressure` sets `P`. `operation_mode.analytical_type` and `uniform_flow` are refused, naming the replacement. The domain is the case's grid: `TGV3D` no longer assigned its own. `config/solvers/Analytical-Zero.yml`, `Analytical-UniformFlow.yml` and `Analytical-TGV3D.yml` are one `Analytical.yml`. `FaceFlux` interpolation works with every analytical field, and a study sweeps a uniform stream through `case.properties.initial_conditions.source.constants.U`.
- Fixed: an expression beginning with `-` and a letter stopped the run, on an inlet, an initial condition or a particle field, because PETSc read it as a second option. Taylor-Green boundary values on the x and y faces evaluated `sin(k*x)` with a loop index in place of the wavenumber.
