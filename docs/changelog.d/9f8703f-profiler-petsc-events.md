- Experimental: PICurv's profiler times functions as PETSc log events. Functions called once or a few times per step are always timed; per-particle and per-cell helpers are timed only when `profiling.helpers` in monitor.yml names them (or is `all`), which removes most of the profiler's cost from particle runs (it was 15-30% of step time). `ProfilingSummary_*.log` keeps its table and adds a section for PETSc's own events, and PICurv's functions now appear in `log_view`.
